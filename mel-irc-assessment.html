Scoring Guide

1 · Poor
No systems or understanding in place; significant gaps exist.
2 · Basic
Some elements exist but informal, inconsistent or poorly implemented.
3 · Adequate
Acceptable structure and understanding; some improvements needed.
4 · Strong
Fully established systems or clear understanding; best practices followed.
